Communication Protocols

Email Standards

  • Format Selection
    Utilize firstname.lastname@[country]un.int for Mission delegates. Use department@icao.int for Secretariat.
  • Subject Line Syntax
    Include reference IDs, meeting numbers, or specific working paper titles (e.g., "A42-WP/103 - Query regarding Annex 17").
  • Language Requirements
    English is the primary operational language. Formal documentation may require French, Spanish, Russian, Arabic, or Chinese.
  • Response Timelines
    Standard processing: 5-7 business days. Technical queries: 2-3 weeks.
